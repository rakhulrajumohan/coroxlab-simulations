🧠 Quick Learning

📏 Distance — how far an object travels.

⏱️ Time — how long the motion takes.

🏃 Speed — how fast an object moves.

Speed = Distance ÷ Time

🐢 Smaller speed → slower motion

⚡ Greater speed → faster motion

Example: 100 m ÷ 10 s = 10 m/s
