📊 How Does It Work?

🚗 Higher speed → Longer stopping distance. A faster car has more motion to remove before it stops.
🛣️ More friction → Shorter stopping distance. Dry roads usually provide more grip than wet or icy roads.
🛑 Stronger braking → Shorter stopping distance. Strong braking can slow the car more quickly when the road has enough grip.
