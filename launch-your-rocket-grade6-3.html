🧠 What Did You Discover?

🔥 More thrust → Greater acceleration.
⚖️ More mass → Harder to accelerate.
⛽ More fuel → Longer powered flight.

A rocket must produce enough thrust to overcome its weight. Once it has lifted off, fuel gives its engine energy to keep accelerating for longer.
