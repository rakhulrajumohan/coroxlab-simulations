🧠 Without Soap vs With Soap

💧 Water onlyWater (H₂O) + 🟡 Oil (hydrocarbons)
Oil stays together.
🫧 Soap + waterSoap (fatty acid salt) + Oil + Water (H₂O)
Soap helps oil be carried away.
Why?

Soap can interact with both water (H₂O) and oily dirt (hydrocarbons). Its oil-loving end interacts with oil, while its water-loving end faces the water.

🧪 Soap vs Detergent

🫧 Soap: a sodium or potassium salt of a fatty acid; helps remove oily dirt from surfaces.

🧴 Detergent: a synthetic surfactant; also helps remove dirt and grease and is commonly used for washing clothes and dishes.

💧 Water (H₂O) + 🧂 dissolved minerals
Hard water contains dissolved minerals such as calcium (Ca²⁺) and magnesium (Mg²⁺). Soap can react with these minerals and form a curdy substance, making cleaning less effective.
