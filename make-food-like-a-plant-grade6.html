💡 Quick Learning

Photosynthesis

Plants make their own food through a process called photosynthesis.

They need:

  • ☀️ Sunlight
  • 💧 Water
  • ☁️ Carbon dioxide
  • 🍃 Chlorophyll

The plant produces:

  • 🍚 Food
  • 💨 Oxygen

Remember: Sunlight + Water + Carbon Dioxide → Food + Oxygen

Leaves are green mainly because they contain chlorophyll.

🍃 Why are leaves green?

Leaves contain a green pigment called chlorophyll. Chlorophyll helps the plant absorb light energy for photosynthesis, and it makes most leaves appear green.

Sunlight is a mix of colours. Chlorophyll absorbs mostly red and blue light and reflects green light, so our eyes see a green leaf.
