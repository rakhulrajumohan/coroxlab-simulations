🧠 What Does Each Part Do?

⚡ Motor Power ↑ → Speed ↑ — more power can make the vehicle accelerate faster.
⚖️ Weight ↑ → Speed ↓ — a heavier vehicle needs more effort to accelerate.
⭕ Larger wheels → more distance per rotation — one rotation covers more ground.
⚙️ Gear ratio changes speed and force — lower gearing favors force; higher gearing favors speed.

Engineering means choosing a combination that fits the job.
