🎯 Engineering Challenges

Challenge 1 — Connect the Tank

Connect the tank to all five houses.

✅ Answer: There's no single correct layout here — any network works as long as every house turns green! A good strategy: build a straight line of pipes across the row the Water Tank is on until you're one column away from the houses. Then use Elbow and T-Junction pieces to turn the line up and down so branches reach every house, finishing each branch with an Elbow that turns into the house.

Challenge 2 — Fewest Pipes

Supply every house using 14 pipes or fewer.

✅ Answer (12 pipes — the fewest possible!):
1. Build a straight trunk from the Water Tank across its row, from column 2 to column 7 — 6 pipes.
2. At column 8, build a vertical line from row 1 down to row 5 — 5 pipes. This passes right by House 1, House 2, and House 3, so branch a connection east into each one as it goes by.
3. From the bottom of that line (column 8, row 5), add one last pipe at column 9, row 5 — the only free spot in the house column. It touches House 4 above and House 5 below, feeding both at once!
Total: 6 + 5 + 1 = 12 pipes.

Challenge 3 — No Broken Pipe

Connect every house without leaving a broken pipe in the active network.

✅ Answer: This isn't about one exact layout — it's about being tidy. After you press SUPPLY WATER, compare 🔵 Pipes Connected with 📏 Pipes Used in the Status panel. If Pipes Used is higher, you've got a leftover pipe that isn't part of the working path — long-press it to remove it, then test again.

Challenge 4 — Supply Quickly

Build a complete network and supply all houses within 30 seconds.

✅ Answer: The clock starts the moment you pick this challenge, so plan first! Use the 12-pipe layout from Challenge 2's answer — since you already know exactly where every pipe goes, you can place them quickly one after another: tap a pipe type, tap the cell, repeat, without stopping to rotate one at a time.
Pick a challenge, build your network, then press 💧 SUPPLY WATER.

🧠 How Water Reaches the Houses

💧 Tank → Main Pipe → Branch Pipes → Houses
Water can travel only through connected pipe sections. If a pipe is broken or points the wrong way, water stops there. Engineers design pipe networks so every home gets a reliable supply.
