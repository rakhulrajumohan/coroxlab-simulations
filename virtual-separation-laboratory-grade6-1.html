🧪 Virtual Separation Laboratory

Choose the best method to separate each mixture.

This simulation was created by COROX LAB

ℹ️ About This Lab

Every mixture is made of two things that need to be separated. Look at the mixture, think about what makes its two parts different, and pick the method scientists actually use.

🎯 Goal

Correctly identify the separation method for all 10 mystery mixtures.

📝 How to Play

  1. Read the mixture name and look at the two icons shown.
  2. Tap one of the method buttons below it.
  3. ✅ Correct — you'll see a short separation animation and the matching answer.
  4. ❌ Wrong — you'll get a hint. Read it and try again; there's no limit on attempts.
  5. Once you're correct, tap Next ➜ to move to the next mixture.

🏆 Scoring

A point is only awarded if you get the answer right on your first try for that question. Guessing wrong first still lets you continue and learn, but that question won't count toward your score.

The score shown at the top (Score: Y / Z) is your points out of the questions you've fully completed so far. Your final score out of 10 appears at the end.

🔀 Random Order

The method buttons are shuffled into a new random order every question, so the correct answer isn't always in the same spot.

🧠 Quick Reference

Tap Show Quick Reference at any time to reveal all 10 methods and the mixtures they match. Tap it again to hide it.

🔄 Reset

Tap Reset at any time to start over from Question 1 with your score cleared.
