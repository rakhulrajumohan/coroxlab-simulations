🧠 Quick Learning

Acids

Show acidic properties. Examples: 🍋 Lemon juice, 🍶 Vinegar.

Bases

Show basic properties. Examples: 🧼 Soap solution, 🧂 Baking soda solution.

Neutral

Neither acidic nor basic. Example: 💧 Water.

🌈 Indicator

An indicator is a substance that changes colour to help us identify whether a solution is acidic or basic.
