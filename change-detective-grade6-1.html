🧠 Quick Learning

🟢 Physical Change

A change where no new substance is formed.

Examples: Melting • Freezing • Evaporation • Condensation • Melting chocolate

🔴 Chemical Change

A change where one or more new substances are formed.

Examples: Burning • Rusting • Cooking

🔄 Remember

Physical changes are often reversible. Chemical changes are often difficult or impossible to reverse.
